The question that started this

Roger's four pears

12. Roger placed four pears in four identical containers. Containers A and C were placed in a room of temperature 4°C while containers B and D were placed in a room of temperature 30°C. Substance X absorbed water from the surroundings. (The four containers are redrawn in Four containers below.)

Roger noticed that mould first appeared on the pear in container D. Explain why mould first appeared on the pear in container D. [2]

The answer given (written as the student wrote it):

The temprature and water was the perfect enviroment for in D mould to grow. container D as it does not have substance x and is warmer than cotainer C. So the mould will grow well in container D

What a 2-mark answer needs. (1) The warmth: D is at 30°C and mould grows well in warm places. (2) The water: D has water next to the pear, so the pear is damp, and mould needs water. B is also warm but Substance X takes in the water, and C has water but is at 4°C. The answer above names temperature and water, but only as “the perfect environment”. It does not say what Substance X does, and it does not say that water is what B is missing.

What fungi like

Mould on bread, mushrooms and the fungi that grow on skin are all fungi. Fungi grow best when four things are there: it is warm, it is dark, it is damp (there is water) and there is food. Take some of these away and the fungi grow less.

Tap each switch to change what the bread has around it. Watch how much fungi grow after 3 days.

  • Warm. Heat helps fungi grow. Cool places slow them down.
  • Dark. Fungi grow well where light cannot reach. Light slows them down.
  • Damp. Fungi need water. Dry places slow them down.
  • Food. Fungi feed on dead things such as dead skin, dirt and old bread. A clean surface gives them nothing to feed on.

Key idea. To reduce fungi, take away what they like: warmth, darkness, water or food. You do not have to take away all four. Taking away even two slows them a lot.

Beyond syllabus How fungi spread, and why they need dead food

This is not required for PSLE. Fungi spread by tiny spores that float in the air, so a loaf can grow mould even in a clean kitchen. Fungi cannot make their own food, so they break down dead things around them. That is why dead skin, dead leaves and old bread are food to fungi.

Four containers

The four pears, containers, and air are the same. Only two things change: the room temperature and what is next to the pear. Substance X takes in water from the air, so the pear next to it stays dry. A beaker of water makes the air damp.
