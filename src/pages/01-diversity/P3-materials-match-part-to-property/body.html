Job first, then the table

The tick table tells you what each material can do. It cannot tell you what each part needs. You have to work that out from the part's job.

  1. What does this part have to do all day? Does it fold, carry a load, sit in the rain, let light in?
  2. Which properties does that job need? Write the list.
  3. Which material has every property on the list? Only then look at the ticks.
If the part…it must be…
bends, folds, wraps or moves with youflexible
meets rain, water or wet ground, or holds waterwaterproof
takes a load, a weight or a pushstrong
has to let daylight inable to let most light through

Key idea. Settle the part with the longest job list first. It usually rules out two options at once. A material with every tick is only needed where every property is needed.

Beyond syllabus Real shoe soles

Real soles are also chosen for grip and for how slowly they wear away. Those are not properties in this topic, so never use them in a tick-table question. The answer must come from the table you are given.
