Five properties, five tests

You cannot tell a property by looking. You find it by doing something to the material and watching what happens.

PropertyWhat you do to the materialIt has the property if…
StrengthHang a load on itit holds the load without breaking
FlexibilityBend itit bends without breaking
WaterproofPut it in water, then measure its mass againit does not absorb water, so its mass does not change
Ability to floatPut it in waterit stays on or near the surface instead of sinking
How much light passes throughShine a torch through it onto a screenmost light, some light or no light reaches the screen

Key idea. Strong means it can take a load without breaking. Flexible means it can bend without breaking. They are two different tests, and a material can pass one and fail the other. Shaping, folding and wrapping are always bending jobs.

Beyond syllabus Words you may see in other books

Some books call a material that lets most light through transparent, some light translucent, and no light opaque. A material that snaps instead of bending is sometimes called brittle. You never need these words: "lets most / some / no light through" and "not flexible" are the syllabus wording.
