Ms Tan's toy globe

17. Ms Tan purchased a toy globe which was resting on a stand. She noticed that the stand was made of a single material. When she flipped the stand upside down, the toy globe floated above the stand. Based on the observation, what could Ms Tan conclude?

① Side K facing up: the globe rests on the stand ② Stand flipped, side L facing up: the globe floats above the stand

Answer key under review — this question is not scored here. The recorded key is (1) and the answer given was (3). By the syllabus, a floating globe means the stand pushes the globe away, and only a magnet can push another magnet away — so both objects must be magnets, which no option says plainly. Key idea

Attract tells you little; repel tells you a lot. A magnetic material is only ever pulled towards a magnet, never pushed away. So if two objects push each other apart, both of them are magnets. An object resting on a stand shows nothing about magnets at all: any solid stand holds an object up.
