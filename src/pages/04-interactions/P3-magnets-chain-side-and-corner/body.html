Beside a magnet, or past its end?

A needle's N end is pushed away from a N pole and pulled towards a S pole. Where the compass sits decides which pole wins, or whether they work together.

Tap each compass, or its button, to see which way the needle points and why. Then flip the magnet and try again.

Back to the question. The compass at R sits beside magnet 1, near R. It does not point at R: it lies alongside the magnet, N end down. A sits beside the right-hand magnet in the same way. That is why the answer is not (4).

Key idea

Unlike poles meet wherever magnets stay together. That holds end to end, side by side (the magnets lie opposite ways round), and at a corner (the end of one magnet against the other magnet's pole). A compass beside a magnet lies alongside it, N end towards the S pole. Only a compass past the end points straight towards or away from the magnet.
