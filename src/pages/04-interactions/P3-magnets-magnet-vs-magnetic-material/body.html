What two objects can do beside each other

Put any two objects side by side and only three things can happen: they attract (pull together), they repel (push apart), or nothing happens.

  • Only magnets repel. If two objects repel, both are magnets.
  • Attract tells you at least one of the pair is a magnet. Two magnets attract when unlike poles face each other.
  • A magnet attracts every iron or steel block. Two plain iron or steel blocks do not pull each other.
  • Two magnets beside each other always push or pull. No force means at least one of them is not a magnet.

Key idea. Start with any pair that repels: both are magnets. Then check every option against every result, not just one.
