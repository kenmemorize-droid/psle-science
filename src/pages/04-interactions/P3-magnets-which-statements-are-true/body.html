The question that started this

Four statements about magnets

21. Which of the following statement(s) is/are true about magnets?

  • A Unlike poles of two magnets will attract each other.
  • B The strength of a magnet does not depend on its size.
  • C All metals can be made into magnets using the electrical method.
  • D Dropping a magnet from a height many times can cause a magnet to lose its magnetism.
  1. (1) A and C only
  2. (2) A and B only
  3. (3) A, B and D only
  4. (4) B, C and D only

Answer: (3). A is true. C is false: only iron or steel can be made into a magnet. B and D are true, but they are not in the syllabus LO list for P3 Magnets, so they are explained under Beyond syllabus below.

Test statements A and C in the labs below.

See it

Statement A: unlike poles

Choose which pole of the right-hand magnet faces the left-hand magnet. Watch what the two magnets do.

Statement C: all metals?

The electrical method makes a magnet by coiling wire around the object and joining the wire to a battery. The stroke method rubs the object with a magnet. Try both methods on different objects, then test what you made with paper clips.

Choose an object and a method. Then tap “Make it, then test it”.

Key idea. Unlike poles (N and S) attract and like poles (two N or two S) repel. A magnet can only be made from iron or steel, not from every metal. Aluminium and copper are metals, but they cannot be made into magnets.

Beyond syllabus Statements B and D: strength and losing magnetism

These two ideas are not in the P3 Magnets LO list, but statement B and D were tested in the school paper.

D. A magnet can lose some of its magnetism. Dropping it many times, hitting it hard or heating it makes it weaker. Then it picks up fewer paper clips.

Tap “Drop the magnet” a few times. This is an illustration, not measured data.

B. The size of a magnet alone does not decide its strength. A small magnet that is made well can pick up more paper clips than a large magnet that is weak. How strong a magnet is depends on how well it is made and how it has been treated, so size alone does not tell you.
