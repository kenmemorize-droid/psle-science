Why each ring ends up where it is (top to bottom)

Key idea

Look at each gap. The ring just above a gap must be a magnet, pushed up by a like pole below it. That push still acts through a non-magnetic ring in between. A magnetic material is only ever pulled by a magnet, so it can never float above one.
  • (2) is possible. Magnet 2 pushes magnet 1 up through the non-magnetic ring when like poles face each other.
  • (3) is not possible. The ring just above the gap is the magnetic material. Magnet 1 pulls it down, so there can be no gap.
Beyond syllabus Can anything block a magnet’s push or pull?

Wood, plastic, paper, glass and water do not stop a magnet’s push or pull. A thick sheet of iron or steel placed between a magnet and an object can weaken it a lot. This is called magnetic shielding. The syllabus says magnetic shielding is not required, so it is not tested on this page.
