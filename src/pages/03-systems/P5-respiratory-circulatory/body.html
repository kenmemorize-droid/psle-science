Breathing and blood in five ideas

1 What air is made of

Air is a mixture of gases: mostly nitrogen, then oxygen, and smaller amounts of carbon dioxide and water vapour.

The air we breathe out is not empty of oxygen. It has less oxygen, more carbon dioxide and more water vapour than the air we breathe in. That is why a cold mirror mists up when you breathe on it.

2 The respiratory system
PartWhat it does
NoseTakes in air, and filters, warms and moistens it.
WindpipeA tube that carries air between the nose and the lungs. It does not take oxygen into the blood.
LungsWhere oxygen passes into the blood and carbon dioxide passes out of the blood.

Breathing in: nose → windpipe → lungs. Breathing out is the same path in reverse.

3 The circulatory system
PartWhat it does
HeartPumps blood around the body.
Blood vesselsTubes that carry the blood to every part of the body.
BloodCarries digested food, oxygen and carbon dioxide.

The heart pumps. The blood vessels carry. The blood is what is carried in them.

4 Three systems working together

The digestive system breaks food down into digested food. The respiratory system takes in oxygen. The circulatory system carries both of them to every body cell.

At the cells, oxygen is used to release energy from digested food. Carbon dioxide is made there, and the blood carries it back to the lungs to be breathed out.

5 Humans, fish and plants
  • Humans take in oxygen through the lungs and give out carbon dioxide there.
  • Fish use gills to take in oxygen dissolved in the water and give out carbon dioxide into it.
  • Plants take in oxygen and give out carbon dioxide through tiny openings in their leaves.
  • Transport: in humans, blood vessels carry digested food, oxygen and carbon dioxide. In plants, one set of tubes carries water from the roots and another set carries food from the leaves.

Beyond syllabus Inside the lungs and the heart

The lungs are not hollow bags: each one ends in millions of tiny air sacs, and the heart is divided into separate chambers with valves that stop the blood flowing backwards. The named parts of both are not required at PSLE, and naming them earns no extra marks.

For PSLE, the parts you need are nose, windpipe and lungs for the respiratory system, and heart, blood and blood vessels for the circulatory system.
