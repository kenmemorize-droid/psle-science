Simplified model: the dots are drawn far larger than real air particles, and volumes are scaled to a 500 mL bottle.

Key idea. Air is matter: it has mass and takes up space. An "empty" bottle is full of air. Air can be squashed into less space, but the more it is squashed, the harder it pushes back. Give it a way out and it moves out of the way.
