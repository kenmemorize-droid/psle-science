Life cycles in three ideas

1 A flowering plant's life cycle

A flowering plant goes through these stages: seed → young plant → adult plant → seed

  • Seed: a new plant starts from a seed.
  • Young plant: a root grows out, then a shoot. The young plant has roots, a stem and leaves, but no flowers yet.
  • Adult plant: it grows bigger and makes flowers. The flowers become fruits with seeds inside.

The seeds can grow into new plants, so the stages repeat. That is why it is a life cycle.

Different plants have different life cycles. A green bean plant goes from seed to flowers in a few weeks; a mango tree takes years.

2 Animal life cycles: 3 stages or 4

Every animal life cycle here starts with an egg and reaches an adult, which lays eggs. Then the cycle starts again.

  • The young plant first feeds on food stored in its seed leaves, until its true leaves can make food.
  • Flowers are where pollination and fertilisation happen.
