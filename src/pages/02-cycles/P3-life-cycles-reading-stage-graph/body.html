The question that started this

Organism T

12. The graph shows the number of days organism T spends at each stage of its life cycle.

Based on the information, which of the following statements is true about organism T?

  1. (1) It has a nymph stage in its life cycle.
  2. (2) It is at its young stage on the fifth day.
  3. (3) It lives for twenty days after it is hatched from the egg.
  4. (4) It takes fifteen days to become an adult after it is hatched from the egg.

Answer: (4). Only (4) can be worked out from the graph. The other three are tested below.

Tap the bars and slide along the days below to find out why (1), (2) and (3) do not work.

See it

Bars, then days

Each bar shows how many days organism T spends in one stage. The stages follow one after the other, so to find a day on the life cycle you put the bars end to end.

Tap a bar to see how long that stage lasts.

Now slide along the days. Day 0 is the day the egg is laid.

Test the four choices

Tap a statement to test it on the graph.

Key idea. A bar is the time spent in one stage. To find how long it takes to reach a stage, add the bars before it. To find the stage on a certain day, add the bars one by one until you pass that day. Then use only what the graph shows: it names the stages Egg, Young and Adult, but it does not say the young stage is a nymph.
