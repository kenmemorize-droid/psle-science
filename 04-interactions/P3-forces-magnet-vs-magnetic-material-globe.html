Theme: InteractionsTopic: Interaction of forces (Magnets)Level: P3

The floating globe: magnet or magnetic material?

Fixes: choosing “magnetic material” for an object that pushes another object away.

  • LO 1: Identify the characteristics of magnets — unlike poles attract and like poles repel; magnets attract magnetic materials.
  • LO 2: Compare magnets, non-magnetic materials and magnetic materials.

Syllabus note honoured: magnetic induction and magnetic shielding are not required, so they are not used here. Standard Science.

1 · What Ms Tan saw

Ms Tan has a toy globe and a stand. The stand is made of a single material.

① Side K facing up: globe rests on the stand ② Stand flipped, side L facing up: globe floats above the stand

Think first: resting and floating are not the same clue. Which of the two tells you more about magnets? You will test this below.
